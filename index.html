01 / ABOUT

Curious by nature.
Practical by design.

Building technical confidence through structured learning, repeatable labs, and clear documentation.

Networks, systems, and the reasoning behind them.

I’m building my expertise in network engineering through a combination of technical study, hands-on lab work, and systematic troubleshooting. My work focuses on understanding how networks are designed, how devices communicate, and what happens when connectivity breaks down. I practise IPv4 subnetting, VLANs, switching, routing protocols, and network troubleshooting in Cisco Packet Tracer, documenting configurations and testing connectivity to validate what I learn. With a foundation in web development, I also understand the application layer and its relationship with the underlying network infrastructure. My direction is clear: develop strong networking fundamentals, gain practical operational experience, and progress toward network security and defensive cybersecurity. I value technical accuracy, clear documentation, and solutions that are reliable in practice — not just correct on paper.

I also have a web-development foundation, which helps me understand applications and the systems they depend on. Over time, I want to build on networking fundamentals and move deeper into network security and defensive cybersecurity.
